UniBordima.lk

UniBordima.lk is a website where Sri Lankan university students can find and rent boarding houses near their universities. Students can: Search & browse nearby boarding/rental listings View listing details, photos & location Save listings they like Leave reviews on places they've stayed Landlords/Owners can: Post their boarding rooms/houses with photos Manage their listings How it works: Register with email → verify with OTP code Browse listings or post your own Contact landlords directly Think of it as a local Sri Lanka version of Airbnb, but specifically for university students looking for long-term boarding accommodation.

AutoRentHub is a MERN stack web app for renting cars and bikes. Users can browse vehicles, book them, pay via Stripe, and leave reviews. Admins can manage vehicles, bookings, users, and view analytics. Backend runs on port 5036, frontend on port 3007.

UniLance

UniLance is a university-exclusive freelance marketplace built for Sri Lankan students. Student freelancers register with a verified .lk university email, validate their skills through an AI-powered MCQ quiz (SmartQuest), and offer services to buyers. Buyers browse gigs, place orders, and pay securely via Stripe. The platform includes order tracking, community forums, payout management, and an admin dashboard — built with React, Node.js, MongoDB, and Google Gemini AI.
